Some words about me

I'm working as Analytics Specialist for Thar share Brokers Private Limited in Jaipur, India, where I work on Indian markets data — NSE and BSE quotes, option chains and broker position books. Alongside that I build and maintain 28 open-source tools for Indian traders: stock screeners, an AI options desk, a Pine Script toolkit and a free learning index. As a Software Developer, I enjoy bridging the gap between engineering and design — combining my technical knowledge with my keen eye for design to create beautiful products. The rule every one of these tools follows: every number carries its source, and nothing gets papered over.
I am passionate about research on making digital agents that explore, learn, and act on their own. My current focus is AI agents for market research, MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers, local LLMs, Pine Script, backtesting and strategy research and OCR of broker screenshots.
